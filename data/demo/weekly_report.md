# Weekly Feedback Report | 16 Nov to 22 Nov 2026

**Total reviews: 240** &nbsp; Positive 53% · Neutral 4% · Negative 43% &nbsp; (avg rating 3.4/5)

> Food safety and order-handling issues were the main risks this week, with Peelamedu driving most of the negative feedback.

## Top 3 complaints
1. **Stale and contaminated dishes** (32 reviews)  
   Customers most often complained about stale mutton, spoiled curry or kuzhambu, and hair found in food, with several serious safety reports tied to specific outlets.
2. **Wrong and missing orders** (28 reviews; mostly Swiggy, 79%)  
   Delivery complaints focused on orders marked delivered but not received, wrong items sent, and late food, with Swiggy standing out as the main platform.
3. **Payment failures at checkout** (13 reviews; mostly Swiggy, 62%; mostly Peelamedu outlet, 54%)  
   Customers reported failed payments that were still debited, plus checkout and support problems, with Peelamedu appearing most often in these app complaints.

## 📍 Concentrated complaints

- **Price complaints on Google**: 9 of 13 (69%), although Google carries 28% of all reviews.

## Top praise
1. **Homely, tasty dishes** (54 mentions)  
   Customers praised the kothu parotta, lunch meals, nattu kozhi curry, and Chettinad chicken biryani for strong flavour and a homely taste.
2. **Fast hot deliveries** (33 mentions)  
   Customers praised quick delivery, including orders arriving hot and on time even in the rain.
3. **Polite helpful service** (19 mentions; mostly RS Puram outlet, 53%)  
   Customers praised friendly, quick, and helpful service, with RS Puram mentioned most often in the positive staff feedback.
