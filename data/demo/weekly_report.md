**Total reviews: 240** &nbsp; Positive 52% · Neutral 2% · Negative 46% &nbsp; (avg rating 3.2/5)

> Customer sentiment was split this week, with strong praise for food and fast delivery but repeated complaints about spoiled food, delivery problems, and payment issues.

## Top 3 complaints
1. **Late, cold deliveries** (31 reviews; mostly Swiggy, 74%)  
   Customers said orders arrived late, cold, or at the wrong address, and several examples mention long waits.
2. **Spoiled and unsafe food** (31 reviews)  
   Customers reported bad smells, undercooked items, hair in food, and fish fry incidents that made meals seem unsafe.
3. **Billing and extra charges** (19 reviews; mostly Google, 84%; mostly Tanglish reviews, 74%)  
   Customers complained that bills did not match menu prices, extra charges were added, and refunds were still pending.

## Top praise
1. **Tasty biryani and meals** (53 mentions)  
   Customers praised the food for being tasty, well spiced, and tender, especially the biryani.
2. **Fast hot deliveries** (34 mentions)  
   Customers liked that orders arrived quickly and stayed hot, even in bad weather.
3. **Smooth app ordering** (14 mentions)  
   Customers said ordering through the app was smooth and live tracking was accurate.
